The driver aborts if a file changes during the run.
Design: one interleaved run under P2 (rknn_server threads and NPU interrupts 36, 37, 38 on CPU4, irqbalance stopped, ~/x4/x4_hold.sh in pin mode, frequencies locked). 16 sessions: conditions S-C0 R-C0 S-C01 R-C01 S-C012 R-C012 S-AUTO R-AUTO (S SiLU, R ReLU, C0 CORE_0, C01 CORE_0_1, C012 CORE_0_1_2), the same order in round 1 and round 2, 45 s rest. One session = benchmark_npu.py: 5 eval_perf without perf_debug (latency) then 5 eval_perf with perf_debug=True (per-layer table), --skip-memory.
Realization of a session, exactly as X4 and X8: helper log in pin mode with zero threads off CPU4 inside the session window, at least 99 percent of the NPU interrupts of the session on CPU4. A condition whose two sessions are not both realized is 'chưa kết luận (P2 không thực hiện đúng)'.
Statements:
B (per model, mask CORE_0_1 and CORE_0_1_2): median of the two session medians per condition. drop_ms = median(CORE_0) minus median(mask), drop_pct = drop_ms / median(CORE_0). gap_ms = the larger, over CORE_0 and the mask, of the absolute difference of that condition's two session medians (conservative). The mask is 'nhanh hơn' only when drop_pct is at least 5 percent and drop_ms is larger than gap_ms, otherwise 'không nhanh hơn'. Gate B is the verdict of the ReLU model at CORE_0_1_2. The SiLU result is the control. S1 is the verdict of the SiLU model for CORE_0_1 and CORE_0_1_2.
A (per model, CORE_0_1_2 sessions): a table row is split when at least 2 of its 3 WorkLoad percentages are above 0.0 percent. coverage = sum of Total cycles of split rows divided by the sum of Total cycles of all rows with cycles. The sum of Total cycles from the board table must equal the sum from the build table of the same .rknn (asserted). A is 'đạt' when coverage is at least 50 percent in every session of that model, 'không đạt' otherwise (coverage does not need realized sessions, it is read from the table). Caveat of the standard: perf_debug may disable parallel mechanisms (UG232 p177), A is an indicator, B decides speed.
Descriptive only: ReLU against SiLU latency at each mask (ms, percent), AUTO against CORE_0 (difference and gap), workload and cycles per OpType (G8), the two Resize rows selected by op name (workload, time, G6), CPU ops of the build tables (G3).
No keep or drop statement here: the label of the candidate needs G1 to G4 and is written in the docs. Limit: one run, 2 sessions per condition, one seed per model.
Budget: experiment 1. Experiment 2 only if some condition was not realized.
