Questions: M12 (S2, three contexts one core each, against the best single context), M13 (S3, explicit pipeline, against S2), M3 and M5 (does three processes differ from three threads), M14 (RSS per context), S1 in throughput form.
Model: baseline SiLU seed 0 INT8, weights/rknn_int8/best-rk3588.rknn (sha256 a501f94ee391bab370c141bbc4853eb78c062635b0a01597fe3a9efb0d99e7dd, same file as phase 1). The model is not changed for S2 and S3 because each context runs on one core, so the multi-core support of the activation op does not enter. ReLU is not measured for S2 and S3 (unknown).
Protocol P3 (new, not P2). P2 puts everything on CPU4, which would serialize three contexts and a pipeline. P3: irqbalance stopped, NPU interrupt line 36 on CPU4, 37 on CPU5, 38 on CPU6 (set once by ~/p3/p3_hold.sh, saved affinities restored at the end), the measuring process runs under taskset -c 4-7 (little cores idle), frequencies locked (make board-check LOCK=1), rknn_server untouched (RKNNLite on the board does not use it). P3 is not approved by the user. The one context P2 latency of the same model (G4B run, CORE_0) is reported next to T1 as 1000 divided by latency.
Realization of a run (per run, from its own record and the helper log): every fired interrupt line has at least 99 percent of its count on its assigned CPU (36 CPU4, 37 CPU5, 38 CPU6), the CPU affinity of the process (and of each child process in T7) is inside CPU4 to CPU7, frequency_before and frequency_after report locked, and the helper log inside the run window shows irqbalance inactive and the three affinities unchanged. A run that is not realized makes its configuration 'chưa kết luận (P3 không thực hiện đúng)'.
Configurations (same order in every repetition, 3 repetitions, 1238 frames each, warm-up 20 frames per context cycling over the first 20 cached images, 20 s rest between runs): T1 one context CORE_0, T2 one context CORE_0_1, T3 one context CORE_0_1_2 (these three are the S1 throughput form), T4 two contexts CORE_0 and CORE_1 (extra point), T5 three threads each with a context on CORE_0, CORE_1, CORE_2 (S2), T6 S3 pipeline (pre workers, one NPU worker per context on CORE_0, CORE_1, CORE_2, post workers, queues of 6), T7 diagnostic only: three processes one context each on CORE_0, CORE_1, CORE_2. Images: data/ppe5/processed/images/test (cached_images in the record gives the number actually cached).
S3 worker formula, fixed now: from the first T5 run, effective NPU time per frame e = median T_npu / 3 (p50 of t_npu_ms, T5 run 1). pre workers = min(3, max(1, ceil(median T_pre / e))), post workers = min(3, max(1, ceil(median T_post / e))). Used for all three T6 runs. Limit: this is one formula, not a search, so S3 gains with other worker counts are unknown.
Statements, only for configurations whose three runs are realized. spread of a configuration = maximum minus minimum FPS of its 3 runs. FPS of a configuration = median of the 3 runs.
M12: best S1 = the one of T1, T2, T3 with the highest FPS. S2 is 'hơn' when FPS(T5) is at least 1.05 times FPS(best S1) and FPS(T5) minus FPS(best S1) is larger than the larger of the two spreads, otherwise 'không hơn'.
M13: S3 is 'đáng giữ' when FPS(T6) is at least 1.05 times FPS(T5) and the gain is larger than the larger spread, or when the gain is within the larger spread and median avg_process_cpu_pct of T6 is lower than of T5 by more than the larger spread of that percentage, otherwise 'không đáng giữ'.
M3 and M5 (diagnostic, not a gate): T7 'khác' from T5 when the difference of the FPS is at least 5 percent of FPS(T5) and larger than the larger spread, otherwise 'không phân biệt được'. It does not say why.
M14 (description): peak and after-init RSS of the process for 1 context (T1) and 3 contexts (T5) from rss_mib, and the difference per added context.
Limits: Python threads (GIL behaviour of RKNNLite.inference is unknown unless T7 differs), only the test images are cached, one model, one seed, temperatures are recorded and not used as a condition.
Budget: experiment 1 (3 repetitions). A second experiment only when P3 was not realized in some run.
