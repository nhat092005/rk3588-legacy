Question (U1, U2, U9 of tmp/2026-10-31_latency-unknowns-tracker.md): split the wall time of each run into parts and find which part carries the difference between the slow, mid and fast levels. Position and timing only, no mechanism claim.
Setup: C API path (rknn_perf_bench, CORE_0, zero UINT8 input), irqbalance stopped, frequencies locked, ftrace with trace_clock mono (same clock as the CSV t_us column), per-CPU buffer 32768 KB (read back, abort if not set). Events: irq:irq_handler_entry and irq_handler_exit filtered on irq 36, sched:sched_wakeup filtered on comm rknn_perf_bench, sched:sched_switch filtered on next_comm or prev_comm rknn_perf_bench, power:cpu_idle on all CPUs. A pilot of 7 runs (thread names only, no timing kept) showed rknn_perf_bench is the only thread switched in, about 63 times per run.
Conditions (app CPU / NPU interrupt CPU, names as E2d): A (no pin, default), a1i0, a0i0, a4i0, a1i4, a4i4. Order: A a1i0 a0i0 a4i0 a1i4 a4i4 then reversed (12 blocks), 50 warm-up and 300 timed runs per block, 20 s rest.
Decomposition of a timed run (window [t_us, t_us + wall_us] from the CSV), from the rknn_perf_bench switch events inside the window:
  C = time the thread is on CPU.
  P = time off CPU after a switch-out with prev_state R (preempted while runnable).
  For each off-CPU interval after a switch-out with prev_state S or D (sleeping), ended by a switch-in at s: the ending interrupt is the LAST irq 36 entry e with switch-out time < e <= s. N = e minus the switch-out time, D = s minus e. If no irq 36 entry lies in the interval, the whole interval counts as N and the interval is flagged unpaired.
  Interrupts that arrive while the thread is on CPU, and earlier interrupts in the same off-CPU interval, add nothing to D.
  Per run: T = wall_us, and C, N, D, P are sums over the run. Closure check: |T - (C+N+D+P)| <= 2 percent of T in at least 95 percent of timed runs of a block (2 percent and 95 percent are new numbers, not user approved).
Idle state metrics (same definition in every cell):
  app_idle = deepest power:cpu_idle state (0 WFI, 1 cpu-sleep) the CPU that later switches the thread in entered during the off-CPU interval (from switch-out to switch-in), per sleeping interval. Per run: share of sleeping intervals with app_idle = 1.
  irq_idle = idle state of the interrupt CPU in the idle period that ends right before the ending interrupt entry e, per sleeping interval. Descriptive only.
Realized block: as E2d (app on its pinned CPU in at least 99 percent of runs, A exempt, at least 99 percent of irq 36 entries on the target CPU), plus the closure check, plus no trace overrun during the block (per-block delta of the overrun counters is 0).
