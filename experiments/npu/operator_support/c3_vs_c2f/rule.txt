Question: docs/contents/01-kien-thuc-nen/06-khoi-cau-thanh-mang/02-csp-va-c2f.rst lists two unknowns after the source search: (U1) the accuracy of C2f against C3 on YOLOv8n with PPE5, (U2) the latency of C2f against C3 on RK3588 and how much of it is the Split and Concat the C2f page attributes to the block. The only measured C3 versus C2f figure found (SL-YOLO, VisDrone, one run) shows +1.0 mAP50 for C2f with +1.5M parameters and no speed. This experiment measures the effect of the block only. It does not settle why Ultralytics designed C2f that way (U0), no run can.
Arms: A = C2f, the stock yolov8n.yaml, runs ppe5_yolov8n_scratch_200ep_s0 (exists), _s1, _s2 (new). B = C3, ai/models/yolov8n_c3_scratch/yolov8n_c3_scratch.yaml (every C2f replaced by C3, same repeats 1 2 2 1 1 1 1 1, same widths, scale n, DFL and SiLU kept), runs ppe5_yolov8n_c3_scratch_200ep_s0, _s1, _s2. Both arms: dataset ppe5, 200 epoch, from scratch (no yolov8n.pt, C3 cannot load its tensors), same config fields as ppe5_yolov8n_scratch_200ep.yaml. The control is the scratch run, not ppe5_yolov8n_baseline_100ep_s0 (pretrained, 100 epoch).
Pre-checks done before training (2026-11-12, ultralytics 8.4.154, nc=5): A has 3,011,823 parameters and 8.196 GFLOPs (ultralytics get_flops at 640), B has 2,487,023 parameters (minus 17.4 percent) and 6.885 GFLOPs. Bottleneck add flags of B: True at L2 L4 L6 L8, False at L12 L15 L18 L21, same as A. Bottleneck kernels of B are (1x1, 3x3), of A (3x3, 3x3). So B is a block swap at the same yaml, not an iso-parameter comparison, and a difference can come from the 17.4 percent smaller model.
Accuracy statements (FP32, leaderboard protocol v1, css_test and ppe2286_ext, AP50-95 in pp): N-1 mean over seeds 0 1 2 of each arm, D = mean(B) minus mean(A). N-2 per-class AP50-95 of NoHelmet and Vest (the two classes the project watches). Spread S = max(1.35, largest max-minus-min across the three seeds of either arm), 1.35 being the seed spread of rule_split.txt, so a spread measured here can only raise it.
Accuracy reading table (fixed now): D at most minus S -> C3 loses accuracy at this size. D at least plus S -> C3 gains accuracy. |D| below S -> the two blocks are not distinguishable with three seeds (not "equal"). The reading is made on css_test, ppe2286_ext is secondary and a disagreement in sign is reported as such.
Latency statements: both arms exported INT8 from the seed 0 run with the same calibration list (--calib-from ppe5_yolov8n_baseline_100ep_s0, 300 images, G3 recipe), measured on the board with the on-board C bench (ai/board/bench/rknn_perf_bench.c), clock locked with sudo -n /usr/local/sbin/lock_freq.sh lock, CORE_0, in ONE session, interleaved A B A B for at least 6 rounds each, no other work on the board during the session. L-1 per-round ratio r = median latency B / median latency A, then the median of the rounds and the range. L-2 noise floor = largest max-over-min minus 1 among the rounds of the SAME model. L-3 detail mode (per-layer table) once per arm: count and total time of Split, Concat and Add rows and their share of the layer-time sum.
Latency reading table (fixed now): median r below 1 minus noise floor -> C3 faster. median r above 1 plus noise floor -> C3 slower. otherwise latency not distinguishable in this session. Expectation from the code, not a gate: A has 17 Concat and 9 Split rows (docs page 03-phan-tich-sau-latency-tung-khoi), 8 of each in the C2f blocks. C3 uses one torch.cat of two tensors and no chunk, so B should keep 17 Concat rows and lose 8 Split rows (1 left). Whatever the detail tables show is reported as read, and a count that differs from this expectation is explained from the build log before the latency is reported.
Secondary (reported only, no reading): the same interleave under CORE_0_1_2. Its WorkLoad column tells whether Split and Add run on all three cores, which settles the two contradicting lines of docs/contents/02-nen-tang-rk3588/05-yolo-tren-rk3588/03-tieu-chi-than-thien-npu.rst for this graph only.
