Design: one interleaved run under P2 (rknn_server threads and NPU interrupts 36, 37, 38 on CPU4, irqbalance stopped, helper in pin mode, frequencies locked). 20 sessions, for mask in CORE_0 then CORE_0_1_2: silu relu leakyrelu relu6 relu200, the same order in both rounds, 45 s rest. One session = benchmark_npu.py (5 eval_perf without perf_debug, then 5 with perf_debug=True), --skip-memory. The driver aborts if a model file changes.
Realization of a session as in onboard_g4b: helper log in pin mode with zero threads off CPU4 inside the session window, at least 99 percent of the NPU interrupts on CPU4. A model whose two sessions at a mask are not both realized is 'chưa kết luận (P2 không thực hiện đúng)'.
Statements: B per model: drop_ms = median(CORE_0) minus median(CORE_0_1_2) of the two session medians, drop_pct = drop_ms / median(CORE_0), gap_ms = the larger of the two conditions' session gaps. 'nhanh hơn' only when drop_pct is at least 5 percent and drop_ms is larger than gap_ms. A per model: a row is split when at least 2 of 3 WorkLoad percentages are above 0.0 percent, coverage = sum of Total cycles of split rows over the sum of all Total cycles of the CORE_0_1_2 session, asserted equal to the build table of the same .rknn, 'đạt' when at least 50 percent in every session. G3: CPU rows of the INT8 and FP16 build tables other than InputOperator and OutputOperator, and the fused op names. Descriptive: each model against SiLU at each mask (ms and percent).
Limit: one run, two sessions per condition, one seed per model. No keep or drop statement here: labels need G1 to G4 and are written in the docs (G1 mapping rule of experiments/npu/operator_support/eval_g4/rule_variants.txt).
Budget: experiment 1. Experiment 2 only if a condition was not realized.
