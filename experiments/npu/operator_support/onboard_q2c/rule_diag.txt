Models and build: ppe5_yolov8n_baseline_100ep_s0 and ppe5_yolov8n_relu_100ep_s0, rebuilt by ai/npu/operator_support/diagnose_quant.py exactly like the Ultralytics 8.4.154 INT8 export (normalized-coordinate ONNX, config mean 0, std 255, defaults of the toolkit: quantized_dtype w8a8, algorithm normal, method channel, optimization level 3, the calibration list of baseline seed 0). Images: 5 calibration images (evenly spaced in the seed 0 calibration list) and 5 css_test images (evenly spaced, letterboxed like the evaluator), 10 per model, one accuracy_analysis call per image, simulator (no board).
Statements (descriptive, no threshold, no keep or drop):
D1 per-layer table: mean over the 10 images of the single-layer cosine ('single cos') of every layer row. Report the 10 rows with the lowest mean single cos per model (op, name) and the mean 'entire' cosine of the last output row.
D2 output tensor output0 (shape 1 x 9 x 8400, boxes channels 0 to 3, class channels 4 to 8): between the float graph (golden) and the simulator, per image the cosine over the class channels and over the box channels, then the mean over images. Per image, among anchors with golden max class score at least 0.25 (deployment confidence) the number whose simulator max class score is below 0.25, and among anchors with golden max class score at least 0.001 (evaluation confidence of Protocol v1) the number whose simulator max class score is below 0.001, and the opposite counts (simulator at least threshold and golden below). Mean over images.
D3 the statement 'class channels lose more than box channels' is made only when the class-channel cosine is lower than the box-channel cosine in every one of the 10 images of a model, 'box channels lose more' when it is higher in every image, otherwise 'chưa kết luận'.
Limits: one simulator run per image, error against the float graph not against labels (no mAP here), 10 images, rebuild not byte-identical to the deployed file, the simulator is not the NPU (the board path is measured by mAP in 2c-c).
